    Truck specs

    Max length and max height are per position. Unload order: 1 comes off first. Stacked height is the combined height of the car above and the car below on one stack, which is how the 4 m overall limit plays out in practice. Deck length is the combined length of the three cars on one trailer deck: the middle spot only fits when the cars either side leave room. The middle spots (R2, R5) also need the tilt, which only works when every car on that deck is at or under the tilt height (default 1.55 m, a first guess from your loads: correct it). Fill order decides which spots get used first when the truck is not full. Upper deck headroom is the spare height the planner tries to keep on upper positions. Lohr code is Lohr's own name for the position (B2 = over-cab platform, K2 = trailer upper deck); fill in the others from your truck. Payload defaults come from listings of 2017–18 EHR 200 C2 units (body about 6.9 t, trailer about 9.8 t); check your registration papers. Overhang in your loads is how far cars stuck out front and rear in your 10 real loads, so country rules are applied on top of that.
